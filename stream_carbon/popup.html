Streaming CO₂ Tracker

See the climate impact of your video choices

Ask yourself: Do you really need 4K for background music or podcasts? Lowering quality on long videos makes a real difference!

Every time you choose a lower resolution, you're reducing digital carbon emissions. The extension updates automatically when you change quality or switch videos.

What does the bottle on the right show?
The water bottle on the right shows the estimated CO₂ impact of the video. The more it fills, the higher the carbon footprint. The number inside estimates the equivalent water use.
